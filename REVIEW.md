### Examined and Decided Was Not a Defect
1. **Calendar date and shift time string representations**:
   `date`, `joined_on`, `shift_start`, and `shift_end` are stored as ISO calendar strings (`YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM`) rather than BSON dates. This was examined and verified to be intentional per `DATA_MODEL.md` (calendar dates and times of day represent calendar concepts in IST, avoiding timezone conversion side-effects).
2. **ABSENT and LEAVE documents have null punch times**:
   Records with status `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` have `punch_in: null`, `punch_out: null`, `work_hours: null`, `late_minutes: 0`, and `overtime_minutes: 0`. This is the exact design specified in `DATA_MODEL.md` section 3.
3. **Audit trail history omission on punch-in/out**:
   Punch-in and punch-out operations do not create or append to `history`. This was confirmed to be strictly by design: `history` is reserved solely for manual corrections (regularizations via `PATCH`).
